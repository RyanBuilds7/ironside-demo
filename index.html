The artists

Nobody here does everything.

Every studio claims range. We'd rather four people who each do one thing properly than four who all do a passable version of the same four. Tell us what you want and we'll put you with whoever actually owns it — even if that means waiting.

Linework

Rae · booking six weeks out

Single-needle, script, botanical, geometry. Work that lives or dies on whether the line holds in five years — so it's drawn to survive settling, not just to photograph well on day one.

Blackwork

Tom · booking three months out

Ornamental, tribal-derived, solid fill and negative space. Big commitments, mapped across the body before anything starts, usually over several sittings.

Colour realism

Dev · longest consultations we run

Portraits, animals, anything that has to read as a real thing from six feet away. The longest consultations we do, and the ones we're most likely to turn down if the reference isn't good enough.

Traditional

Bea · usually the quickest to get in

Bold line, limited palette, designs that were solved a century ago and haven't needed improving. Fast to sit, ages better than anything else on this list.

The work

Nothing starts on the day you ask.

Every piece runs through the same three stages before a needle touches skin. It's slower to book and it's the reason we don't do cover-ups of our own work.

01

Consultation

Thirty minutes, free, in the studio. Bring references, bring nothing, bring a rough idea you can't describe. We'll tell you honestly if it works on the body part you want it on.

02

Drawing

Your artist draws it and sends it over. You get one round of changes included, and more if it genuinely isn't right yet. Nobody gets stencilled on something they haven't seen.

03

Sitting

Half day or full day, priced by session not by hour, so we're never rushing the last inch to hit a number. Big pieces get booked as a run of dates up front.

The studio

One chair, one client, one day.

No walk-ins, no double-booking, no queue in the doorway watching you get tattooed. Four private rooms off a shared floor in a Digbeth warehouse unit, and if you're the two o'clock, the room is yours until you leave.
